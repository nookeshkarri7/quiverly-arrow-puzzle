Quiverly: Arrow Puzzle Escape

An arrow puzzle about order. Every arrow points somewhere and can only fly out when nothing stands in its way. Tap the free ones out, open up space and clear the board. Free, no timer, plays offline.

Get it on Google Play Visit the website

Features

  • Press an arrow to see its exit line, lift to launch, slide off to cancel.
  • The nearest arrow to your touch is picked, and the first slip on every level is free.
  • Every board fits your phone; pinch to zoom, tap Fit to see it all.
  • Freshly generated levels that grow into dense, twisting logic puzzles, with a chapter bonus every 20 levels.
  • Hints paid with coins you earn by playing, a daily puzzle, and a 7-day check-in.
  • Ads never interrupt a level; watching a video is always your choice.
